## Overview

Two of the biggest names in AI image generation: **Midjourney** and **DALL-E 3** (via ChatGPT). We ran 20+ identical prompts through both to see which one wins.

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Midjourney | DALL-E 3 |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| Price | $10–$60/mo | Included in ChatGPT Plus ($20) |
| Interface | Discord | ChatGPT web |
| Artistic quality | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Prompt accuracy | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Ease of use | ⭐⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Commercial rights | ✅ | ✅ |

## Midjourney: Strengths

- Unmatched artistic, painterly quality
- Incredible for concept art, marketing visuals, mood boards
- V6 model has much better text rendering
- Active community on Discord

**Best for:** Creative professionals, designers, content creators

## DALL-E 3: Strengths

- Easiest to use — just type what you want in ChatGPT
- Much better at following complex instructions
- Great for realistic photos and product mockups
- No separate app needed if you have ChatGPT Plus

**Best for:** Beginners, marketers, bloggers who need quick images

## Head-to-Head: Same Prompts

We tested both with: *"A futuristic city at sunset, cyberpunk style, neon lights"*

- **Midjourney** produced a more cinematic, artistic result
- **DALL-E 3** produced a more literal, clean interpretation

## Verdict

**Choose Midjourney** if: you're a creator who needs stunning, artistic images  
**Choose DALL-E 3** if: you want ease of use and already pay for ChatGPT Plus

**Winner: Midjourney (for quality) | DALL-E 3 (for convenience)**
