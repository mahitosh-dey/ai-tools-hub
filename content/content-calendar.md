## QUICK SEO TIPS FOR EACH POST

1. Put main keyword in: Title, first paragraph, one H2, URL slug
2. Add a comparison table in every review/comparison post
3. Add a "Quick Answer" box at the top (gets Google featured snippets)
4. Internally link every new post to 2-3 older posts
5. Aim for 1,500+ words minimum — Google prefers depth
6. Add real screenshots of tools you're reviewing

---

## KEYWORDS TO AVOID (Too Hard Right Now)

- "AI tools" (too broad, dominated by Forbes/TechCrunch)
- "ChatGPT" alone (100M+ competing pages)
- "artificial intelligence" (impossible to rank)
- "best AI" (too vague)

Focus on SPECIFIC long-tail keywords until month 6+
