## Recording Tips

- **Long video:** Screen record + voiceover. Free tools: OBS (PC/Mac) or Loom. No face camera needed to start.
- **Shorts:** Record vertically on your phone. Talk directly to camera. One take is fine.
- **Editing:** CapCut (free) or DaVinci Resolve (free).
- **Always add captions** — 80% of Shorts are watched with sound off.
